## How I tested

- Installed the HisabDo mobile app on my device.
- Explored the major features: Dashboard, Customers, Transactions (Receivable/Payable), Categories, Reports/Settings.
- Introduced the app to 5 real users and collected their genuine feedback.

## My own feedback

| Area | Feedback / Suggestion | Issues Found | Recommended Improvement |
|------|----------------------|--------------|-------------------------|
| Dashboard | Clean and simple, good overview of receivables and payables at a glance. | No graphical insights; only totals shown, no trend over time. | Add a simple bar/line chart of monthly receivable vs payable, plus a summary card showing this month's cash movement. |
| Receivable / Payable | Core khata tracking works well; adding transactions is fast and intuitive. | No way to see which khata entries are overdue; no reminder for pending amounts. | Add due-date reminders and an "overdue" filter, plus a one-tap "mark as paid" action from the list. |
| Customers | Customer list is clear and searching is easy; new customers can be added quickly. | No customer stats shown, and deleting a customer with history is blocked without explanation. | Show each customer's total receivable/payable balance on the list and explain why a customer with transactions cannot be deleted. |
| Reports | Basic reporting exists but is limited in scope. | No export options; cannot share or print the report directly from the app. | Add PDF/Excel export and share/print support so shopkeepers can send khata statements to customers. |
| General | App is lightweight, easy for non-technical shopkeepers to use, and works offline well. | No backup/restore option; settings are limited to currency and language; if the phone is lost, data is lost too. | Add local backup (export/import) and cloud backup later; expand settings with profile details and receipt/statement branding. |
